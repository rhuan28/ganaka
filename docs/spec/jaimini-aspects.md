# Jaimini aspects and Moon and Sun references

## Aspects between signs

- A **movable** sign (Aries, Cancer, Libra, Capricorn) aspects the fixed
  signs, except the next neighbour.
- A **fixed** sign (Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, Aquarius) aspects the movable ones,
  except the previous neighbour.
- A **dual** sign (Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius, Pisces) aspects the other
  duals.

## What PL9 lists

- **Planets in movable and fixed signs:** each planet (from Sun to Ketu) in a
  movable sign, with each planet in a fixed sign that its sign aspects; the
  planet in the movable sign comes first.
- **Planets in dual signs:** the planets in dual signs, when there are at
  least two — including if they are all in the same sign, in which case they do not
  aspect one another.

## Moon and Sun references

The house of each point counted from the sign of the Moon (Chandra lagna) or of the
Sun (Surya lagna), that sign being house 1.

## Origin

Classical aspect rule; the form of the two lists was **measured** in the 72
charts printed by PL9 (page "Jaimini System"), with a full match.
