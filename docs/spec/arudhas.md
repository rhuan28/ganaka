# Arudha padas, Arudha Lagna and Upapada

## Pada of a house

`S` is the sign of the house and `R` the sign where the lord of `S` is (Aries and
Scorpio: Mars; Taurus and Libra: Venus; Gemini and Virgo: Mercury; Cancer:
Moon; Leo: Sun; Sagittarius and Pisces: Jupiter; Capricorn and Aquarius:
Saturn).

1. Count how many signs there are from `S` to `R`, and count the same number
   from `R`.
2. **Parashara** (option "Bhavapada calculation: Parashara", the one observed): if
   the result is `S` itself or the 7th from `S`, take the 10th from the
   result.

The padas of the twelve houses are those from the Lagna onward. PL9 also shows them in the
Navamsha and in the Dashamsha, with the signs of those vargas, by the same rule.

## K.N.Rao

With the option "Bhavapada calculation: K.N.Rao", PL9 gives the pada **without** the
rule of step 2: the pure count, the same as the special point "Arudha
Lagna". It applies to the three pada charts (Rashi, Navamsha and Dashamsha). The
option does not change the Arudha Lagna or the Upapada of the special points.

## Arudha Lagna

PL9's special point "Arudha Lagna" is the pada of house 1 **without** the rule
of step 2. The pada of house 1 in the pada chart ("1P") follows the rule; the two
differ when it applies.

## Upapada

The pada of the 12th house, with the rule of step 2, where the 12th house is counted
**backward** when the Lagna is in an even sign: with an odd Lagna it is the sign
before the Lagna; with an even Lagna, the one after.

## Origin

- Pada and Parashara's rule: classical, **confirmed by measurement** against
  the 72 charts printed by PL9 (the 864 padas of the natal chart, and those of the
  Navamsha and the Dashamsha).
- Arudha Lagna and Upapada: **deduced from the numbers** — the direct classical
  hypotheses matched 46 and 39 of 72; the rules above match all 72. They are
  PL9 conventions, not defects: `corrected` mode keeps them.
- K.N.Rao: **measured** on 40 charts printed with the option; of the 1,440 padas,
  467 changed relative to Parashara, all as the pure count. The rule that
  is usually attributed to K.N.Rao (10th when it falls in the house, 4th when it falls in the
  7th) is not the one PL9 applies.
- PL9's routines were not read.

## What was not verified

- Ties or border cases: the padas depend only on the signs.
